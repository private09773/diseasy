# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include
demonstrating empathy, being respectful of differing opinions, and accepting
constructive feedback gracefully.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, trolling, insults, and
publishing others' private information without consent.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
